## Preamble

This Stock Purchase Agreement is made as of 8 June 2026 among Helios Seed, Inc.
("Company") and the investors listed on Schedule A ("Purchasers").

## 1. Definitions

"Preferred Stock" means the Series Seed Preferred Stock of the Company.

## 2. Purchase and sale

Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Purchasers will purchase 2,000,000
shares of Preferred Stock at USD 0.50 per share.

## 3. Intellectual property assignment

Founders represent that they have assigned to the Company all intellectual
property used in the business. Company owns all work made for hire by employees.

## 4. Confidentiality

The parties shall keep term sheets and capitalization data confidential until
a public filing or mutual written consent.

## 5. Indemnification

The Company shall indemnify Purchasers against losses from a breach of the
Company's representations, subject to a basket of USD 25,000.

## 6. Limitation of liability

Except for fraud, the Company's aggregate indemnification cap equals the
purchase price actually paid by the claiming Purchaser.

## 7. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
