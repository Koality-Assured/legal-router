## Preamble

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 15 April 2026
between Acme Analytics LLC ("Customer") and Northwind Software, Inc. ("Vendor").

## 1. Definitions

"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by a party.
"Services" means the hosted analytics platform described in Order Forms.
"Security Incident" means unauthorized access to Customer personal data.

## 2. Fees and payment

Customer shall pay fees within forty-five (45) days of invoice. Late amounts
accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month.

## 3. Intellectual property assignment

Vendor hereby assigns to Customer all intellectual property in custom work
product created solely for Customer. Pre-existing Vendor tools remain Vendor's.
Vendor waives moral rights in custom work product to the extent permitted by law.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using at least
reasonable care and shall not disclose it except to personnel with a need to know.
Vendor shall notify Customer of a Security Incident within seventy-two (72) hours.

## 5. Indemnification

Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer from third-party
claims alleging that the Services infringe a U.S. patent or copyright, subject
to prompt notice and exclusive control of the defense. Indemnity does not apply
to claims arising from Customer materials.

## 6. Limitation of liability

Except for indemnity obligations, confidentiality breaches, and data protection
breaches, each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed two times (2x) the
fees paid in the twelve (12) months before the claim. Neither party is liable
for consequential damages except for unauthorized disclosure of personal data.

## 7. Service levels and credits

Vendor targets 99.95% monthly uptime. If monthly availability falls below 99.9%,
Customer receives a service credit equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of that
month's fees. Credits are the exclusive remedy for downtime.

## 8. Data protection

Vendor is a processor of Customer personal data and shall implement appropriate
technical and organizational measures consistent with GDPR Article 28 and
Article 32. A data processing addendum is attached as Exhibit A.

## 9. Insurance

Vendor shall maintain cyber liability insurance with policy limits of at least
five million dollars (USD 5,000,000).

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach if uncured after thirty (30) days
written notice. Upon termination Vendor shall return or delete Customer data
within fifteen (15) days.

## 11. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, excluding
conflict-of-law rules. Exclusive venue lies in the state or federal courts of
Wilmington, Delaware.
